🧩 Reasoning: High-Speed Tricks & Patterns

Coding-Decoding, Alphabet Positions, Blood Relations aur Ranking Rules.

1. Alphabet Positions (EJOTY Trick)

Positions 5 ke multiples me yaad rakhein: E(5), J(10), O(15), T(20), Y(25).

Opposite Letters (Sum = 27): A-Z (AZad), B-Y (BoY), C-X (CruX), D-W (Dew), E-V (EVening), F-U (FUll), G-T (GT road), H-S (High School), I-R (Indian Rail), J-Q (Jungle Queen), K-P (KanPur), L-O (LOve), M-N (MAN).

2. Row & Ranking Formulas

  • • Total Persons: Total = Left + Right - 1
  • • Right Position: Right = (Total - Left) + 1
  • • Overlapping Persons: Between = (L1 + R2) - Total - 2

3. Blood Relations Coding

  • • Male: [+] | Female: [-]
  • • Married Couple: Double arrow [A <=> B]
  • • Siblings: Single line [A - B]
  • • Generations: Vertical line [Father | Son]

4. Number Series Patterns

Har series me pehle Difference check karein: Step difference (+2, +4, +6), Difference of Difference, Squares/Cubes (n² ± 1, n³ ± 1), ya alternate series.
