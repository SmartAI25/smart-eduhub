SSC CGL me 100% aane wale Subject-Verb Agreement, Prepositions aur Spelling rules.

1. Top Subject-Verb Agreement Rules

  • • As well as / Along with / With / In addition to: Verb hamesha First Subject ke anusar aati hai. (e.g., The teacher with his students was present).
  • • Neither...nor / Either...or / Not only...but also: Verb hamesha Nearest (Second) Subject ke anusar aati hai.
  • • Each / Every / Either / Neither: Inke sath hamesha Singular Verb use hoti hai. (e.g., Each of the boys has a book).
