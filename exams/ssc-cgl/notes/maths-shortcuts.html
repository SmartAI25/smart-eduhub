📐 Quantitative Aptitude: High-Yield Formulas

SSC CGL Tier-1 me bar-bar pooche jane wale direct shortcuts aur formulas.

1. Profit, Loss & Markup

  • • Markup & Discount: Agar CP par M% markup aur D% discount ho, toh net profit % = M - D - (M × D / 100)
  • • Selling Price (SP): SP = CP × (100 + P%) / 100
  • • Successive Discounts (d1, d2): Net Discount = (d1 + d2 - d1×d2 / 100)%

2. Simple & Compound Interest

  • • Sum Doubling Trick: Agar koi dhan T saal me n guna hota hai, toh m guna hone ka samay = (m - 1) / (n - 1) × T
  • • 2 Years CI - SI Difference: Diff = P × (R / 100)²
  • • 3 Years CI - SI Difference: Diff = P × (R / 100)² × (300 + R) / 100

3. Time, Work & Efficiency

  • • A and B Together: Agar A = x din, B = y din leta hai, toh dono milkar = (x × y) / (x + y) din lenge.
  • • Man-Days Formula: (M1 × D1 × H1) / W1 = (M2 × D2 × H2) / W2
  • • Efficiency Ratio: Efficiency samay (Time) ki ulti (Inverse) hoti hai: E1 / E2 = T2 / T1

4. Speed, Distance & Trains

  • • Unit Conversion: km/h × (5/18) = m/s aur m/s × (18/5) = km/h
  • • Average Speed (Equal Distances): 2 × S1 × S2 / (S1 + S2)
  • • Relative Speed: Same direction = (S1 - S2) | Opposite = (S1 + S2)
